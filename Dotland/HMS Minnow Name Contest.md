### 👑 An Announcement from the Royal Fleet

Some voyages begin with a map. Ours began with a little Dot, a curious fish, and a belief that stories, music, and imagination can take us somewhere extraordinary.

Today, the crew has expressed her **preferred new name** for the Royal Dotland flagship:  (We await her royalties decision.)

The crew in their infernal ineptitude put forth . . . <dot> <dot> <dot>

# **HMS DOTYSSEY** 🔴⚓🐟🌈

> **One little Dot. One extraordinary voyage.**

The name celebrates both where we've come from and where we're going: a continuing *odyssey* through Dotland, the World of Color, and a growing community of creators, collectors, and friends.

**Important Royal Notice:** This is a **preferred name under Admiralty review**, **not an official renaming**. The vessel remains **HMS Minnow** until the Queen issues an express decree.
