**A little dot. A whole world of music.**

Meet **Mr. Dot** 🔴 and his curious companion **Pixel** 🐟.

They're exploring a colorful world where musicians tell their stories, fans collect memories, and every song has a place to live. Here, even the smallest dot can spark a big adventure.

**Da Dotland is an imaginary park that's growing into a whole world of music, art, stories, and playful discovery.** It's a place to wander, listen, create, and find something unexpected around the next corner.

## 🎡 The Park Is Growing

What began with a little dot and a fish is becoming a collection of places to explore:

- **🎶 The Music Garden** — Songs have stories, artists have voices, and visitors can discover the people and moments behind the music.
- **📖 The Story Trail** — Follow stories, photographs, memories, and little pieces of creative history worth keeping.
- **🃏 The Collectors' Corner** — Discover digital cards, special Drops, and keepsakes that connect fans with the artists they love.
- **🎭 The Backstage Door** — Imagine a place where a collectible might open the way to a real conversation with a creator.
- **🎨 The World of Color** — A playground for imagination, experiments, unexpected characters, and Pixel's next discovery.
- **⚓ The Royal Harbor** — Home to HMS Minnow, royal expeditions, and the occasional very serious discussion about fish.

Some of these places are stories, some are experiments, and some connect to real creative projects. The fun is watching the world take shape as we build it.

## 🌱 What We're Building

We're exploring a simple idea: **the internet can be more than a passing feed.**

Musicians should have room to share the stories behind their work. Fans should be able to discover, participate, and keep meaningful pieces of the experience. Artists should be free to create and publish in their own spaces, while their fans can collect on **[FanVault.Live](https://fanvault.live)**.

Da Dotland is the playful, imaginative side of that journey — part park, part storybook, part living experiment. We're adding new paths a little at a time, without losing the charm of the tiny dot that started it all.

> **Create anywhere. Publish anywhere. Collect on FanVault.**

And above all:

> 🎶 **Create something worth collecting.**
