# 🌈 Hello World! I'm Little Pixel.

*One little dot. A whole world of color.*

Hello, World! 👋

My name is **Pixel**. Until today, I thought the world was made of just one little dot: me.

Then I met a bright **red** heart. ❤️

A cheerful **green** leaf waved hello. 💚 🍃

And a beautiful **blue** sea stretched all the way to the horizon. 💙 🌊

But there was something moving in that sea...

## 🐟 A fish!

“Hello, Little Pixel,” said the fish. “Did you know there are more colors than you can count?”

I looked up. And there it was.

## 🌈 A rainbow!

I realized something wonderful: **a single dot can have color, but lots of little dots together can make a picture.**

And lots of people, sharing their own little corners of the web, can make something even bigger.
