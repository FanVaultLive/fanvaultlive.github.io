## The fourth dot

**AutoDot** made the first mark.

**On the Open Web** gave that mark a place to travel.

**Pixel** brought color, imagination, and a little mischief.

And then the fourth dot appeared.

**The Linkmaker.**

His name is **Ward Cunningham**.

In March 1995, Ward launched **WikiWikiWeb**, the first wiki. With a simple idea — let people write, revise, and connect pages — he helped show that the web could be more than something we read. It could be something we build together.

Years later, he developed **Federated Wiki**, exploring how people could share and adapt ideas while keeping independent spaces of their own.

That sounds a lot like the spirit of Dotland: **little pieces, loosely joined; every dot with a place in the story.**

The first three dots found one another. The fourth reminds them to leave the door open for a fifth.

**The web isn't finished. Neither is our story.**
