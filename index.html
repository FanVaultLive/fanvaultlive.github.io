Create something worth collecting

Your first Drop can start with a simple document.

We're using open tools as we build: write your story, save your artwork and sounds, plan a Deck, then create Cards on FanVault. No programming required to begin.

  1. 01 Write a story or plan in Markdown. Or use Google Docs or whatever you are comfortable with.
  2. 02 Gather images, music, and other creative materials. The point is to start organizing your materials all in one place.
  3. 03 Follow the Drop Planning Template to design your Deck and Cards. Use the template to create a plan and to be ready to copy and paste your text and images and sounds into FanVault.Live.
  4. 04 Visit FanVault.Live to create and try your Drop. Once you login click on the Creator tab and you can create decks, add cards, assign images and sounds and special popup text messages as rewards.

Why we're publishing here

The internet can be more than a passing feed.

FanVault explores Cards that hold a song, image, story, memory, or connection to an artist. Dotland is the playful side of that work, and GitHub is our open publishing library.

We write in Markdown, show pages like this in HTML, and link back to the source documents so you can read how they're made. The experiments are public; not every imagined feature is available in the FanVault product today.

Create anywhere. Publish anywhere. Collect on FanVault.
