Make your own drop

A great Drop starts with a simple idea.

Begin with a story, a song, or a collection of images. You can plan everything in Markdown, a document, or a notebook — whatever works for you.

  2. 02 Plan your Deck and Cards with the Drop Planning Template.
  3. 03 Sign in to FanVault.Live, open Creator view, and make your Drop.
